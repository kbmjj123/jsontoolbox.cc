# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JsonToolBox (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and tools at [jsontoolbox.cc](https://jsontoolbox.cc).

By using JsonToolBox, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Data Processing

All JSON and file processing in JsonToolBox happens entirely in your browser. Your data is:

- **Not uploaded** to any server  
- **Not stored** remotely  
- **Not transmitted** over the network for processing  

We have no access to the JSON, files, or any other content you process using our tools. Once you close the page, the data is no longer accessible to us.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We explicitly do **not** collect:

- The content of your JSON, files, or any data you paste into our tools  
- Personal information through tool usage (e.g., names, emails, IDs in your JSON)  
- Any data that could be used to reconstruct your input  

## 3. Analytics

We use privacy-focused web analytics to understand how our site is used. This may include:

- Page views and navigation paths  
- Referrer information (e.g., search engine, social media)  
- Device type, browser type, and approximate location (e.g., country/region)  

This data is anonymized and aggregated. It does **not** include your JSON content or any personal information that could identify you.

## 4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality only, such as:

- Remembering your theme preference (light/dark mode)  
- Remembering your language settings  

We do **not** use cookies for:

- Advertising or ad targeting  
- Cross-site tracking  
- Selling data to third parties  

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features (e.g., theme persistence).

## 5. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third-party services for advertising or tracking purposes. Our tools are designed to work entirely within your browser.

If we integrate any third-party services in the future (e.g., error reporting, additional analytics), we will update this policy and ensure they comply with our privacy principles.

## 6. Your Choices

You can:

- Use JsonToolBox without enabling cookies (via your browser settings)  
- Clear your browser data at any time  
- Stop using the site at any point; no data is retained on our side  

## 7. Children’s Privacy

JsonToolBox is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
